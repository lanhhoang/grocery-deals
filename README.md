# Daily Grocery Deals

A module for ARI that surfaces the best grocery deals near you — every time you refresh, it scans flyers from stores in your area and shows you what's on sale, sorted by biggest discount.

---

## What it does

- **Deals feed** — Browse current grocery deals pulled from Flipp, a flyer aggregator that covers major Canadian chains (Longo's, Farm Boy, T&T, No Frills, and more). Each deal card shows the item, store, product image, current vs. original price, and the discount percentage.
- **Save deals** — Star any deal to keep it in your personal Saved Deals list, so you don't lose track of it between refreshes.
- **Dashboard widget** — A compact card on your ARI dashboard shows the top 5 deals at a glance, so you can spot a great deal without opening the module.
- **Keyword watchlist** — You decide what to track. Add words like "chicken", "eggs", or "olive oil" and the module only fetches deals that match your list.
- **Card or list view** — Switch between a grid of cards (with images) and a compact list, depending on how much you want to see at once.

---

## Getting started

1. Open **Grocery Deals** from the sidebar.
2. On first launch, enter your **postal or ZIP code** — this is used to find deals near you.
3. Go to **Settings** and add a few keywords to your watchlist (e.g. "beef", "eggs", "milk").
4. Hit **Refresh Today's Deals** on the main page. Deals will appear within a few seconds.

---

## Tips

- Deals are **not** updated automatically — click "Refresh Today's Deals" whenever you want fresh results.
- The discount badge is colour-coded: **green** (40%+ off), **amber** (20–39% off), **grey** (under 20% off).
- Duplicates are filtered out automatically — if the same item appears in multiple flyers, only the best deal is shown.
- You can add up to **20 keywords**. Broader terms (e.g. "chicken") return more results than specific ones (e.g. "chicken thighs").
